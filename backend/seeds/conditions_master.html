    3. If the delay continues and the Subcontract Works progress falls (10%) behind the work programme (measured in delayed days relative to the total duration of the Subcontract Works), the Main Contractor shall have the right to terminate the Subcontract Agreement, in accordance with the termination provisions of the Subcontract Agreement.
